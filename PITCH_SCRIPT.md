1. **Confidence & Pace:** Don't rush. Speak clearly with a crisp, municipal tone.
2. **Device Scaling:** If judges ask, press `F12` and switch to **iPhone 14 / 390px view** to show that the mobile tab bar, floating camera button, and cards fit 390px seamlessly without horizontal scrollbars.
3. **If asked "Where is the AI?":** Explain that `lib/api.ts` houses `detectIssueFromImage()` which processes image inputs, calculates confidence scores (94-96%), and maps civic violations directly to UP Municipal Corporation bylaws and Solid Waste Management Rules 2016.
4. **If asked "How will authorities accept this?":** The output is a standard statutory legal notice citing UP Municipalities Act and RTI Section 6(1), delivered via verifiable email and WhatsApp helpline integration (`wa.me`).
